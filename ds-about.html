Our Roots

ប្រទេសកម្ពុជា → ក្រូណា, កាលីហ្វ័រនីញ៉ា

Cambodian Immigrants,
Corona's Own.

Our family came to the United States from Cambodia, carrying little more than hard work, resilience, and the dream of building something lasting. We found our home in Corona, California — and for over 20 years, we've been waking up before the sun to bake fresh donuts for this community we love.

Every donut is a small piece of that journey. From Phnom Penh to Green River Road — this shop is what the American dream tastes like to us. We are proud to be Cambodian-owned, family-run, and forever grateful to the neighbors who've supported us from day one.
