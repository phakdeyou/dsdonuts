Our Story

A little shop
with a big heart.

D's Donuts started with a simple dream: bring the kind of donut shop you remember from childhood back to Corona. No shortcuts, no mass production — just fresh dough, real ingredients, and love poured into every batch.


We're a family operation, and you'll likely see us behind the counter every single morning. Come for the donuts, stay for the community.
